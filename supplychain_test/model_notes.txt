Master Data
UnitOfMeasure
Holds units like kg, L, pack—so every product’s quantity can be interpreted correctly.

Category
Groups products (cold, dry, drinks, bar, etc.) for filtering and reporting.

Supplier
Vendors you purchase from, including contact details.

Product
Your catalog item: SKU, name, cost, UoM, categories, vendors, and any assigned users responsible for it.

Requisition & Approval
Requisition
The “header” for a user’s request: tracks requester, status (pending/approved/denied/queried), evidence attachment and an urgent flag.

RequisitionItem
Each line on the requisition: which product and how many units.

RequisitionApproval
Audit trail of every approval action (who approved/denied/queried, when, and any notes).

Purchase Order (PO) & Approval
PurchaseOrder
Generated from an approved requisition (or created directly), links to a supplier, recorded by “Mr Monday,” then routed to Mum for final sign-off. Tracks its own status lifecycle and “sent” timestamp.

PurchaseOrderItem
The line items on the PO, capturing product, requested quantity, and the snapshot unit cost at time of ordering.

PurchaseOrderApproval
Logs each PO approval/denial/query—who acted, when, and why.

Receiving & Invoice Processing
Receiving
When goods arrive: records which PO, who received them, when, plus the uploaded supplier invoice document.

ReceivingItem
Records actual quantities received against each PO line, and a JSON “flagged_for” object to track per-department sign-off (kitchen, store, audit, admin).

InvoiceLineApproval
Accountant’s line-by-line sign-off on the invoice: approved (or not), notes, timestamp.

Payment
Payment
Final payment record for a PO: one-to-one link, “approved_by_mum” flag, payment type (cheque vs transfer), optional notes, proof file upload, and processed timestamp.

Store Issuance
IssuanceRequest
Internal request from store to kitchen to draw stock; mirrors requisitions but for in-house usage. Tracks requester, status, and uploaded issue slip.

IssuanceItem
Lines on that request: product and quantity issued.

Inventory & Alerts
StockTransaction
Atomic log of every inventory change—both receipts and issues—so you can compute current stock levels by summing.

LowStockAlert
Fires when a product’s real-time stock dips below its threshold; records when it fired and whether someone has acknowledged it.
